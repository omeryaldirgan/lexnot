Frequently asked questions

How do I create a note?

Tap + Note on the home screen. You can also open a folder first and create your note there.

How do I draw?

Open Pen inside a note. Choose between pen, highlighter and eraser, and adjust the color and thickness.

How do I add an audio recording?

Tap Add audio inside your note to record. Microphone permission is required. You can listen to recordings inside the note; recordings are not automatically transcribed.

How do I share a note as a PDF?

Tap PDF in a note, then save or share it using the menu. The PDF contains note text and drawings, but not audio recordings or other attachments.

How do I back up my notes?

Go to Settings → Export backup and save the file somewhere safe. On supported versions, Restore backup adds it to a separate folder. The backup limit is 100 MB.

Do my notes sync across devices?

Lexnot does not currently offer automatic cross-device sync. To move notes, export a backup and restore it on a supported version.

What happens if I delete the app?

Deleting the app may also delete its local Lexnot data. Export a backup and keep it somewhere safe before deleting the app or changing devices.
